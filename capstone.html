Capstone Project

Development and Implementation of a Web and Mobile-Based LPG Delivery Management System with Real-Time Tracking and Automated Order Processing

This system was developed to streamline the LPG ordering and delivery process for customers, sellers, riders, and administrators. It combines a web-based administrative dashboard with a mobile-friendly customer and rider interface to automate order handling, monitor deliveries in real time, and improve the overall efficiency of the LPG business.
